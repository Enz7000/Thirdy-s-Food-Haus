Pickup Information

How Pickup Works

  1. Place your order online

    Browse the menu, add items to cart, and complete checkout with a 50% downpayment.

  2. Receive confirmation

    You'll get an Order ID and a 4-character Pickup Code. Save these!

  3. Wait for verification

    Our team will verify your downpayment. You'll be notified when your order is confirmed.

  4. Visit the Pre-Order Pickup counter

    Go to the designated pickup area at the restaurant on your scheduled date/time.

  5. Show your Order ID or Pickup Code

    Present your Order ID or Pickup Code to the staff at the Pre-Order Pickup counter.

  6. Pay the remaining balance

    Pay the remaining 50% in cash and collect your freshly prepared order.
